How to Buy Car Spare Parts in Saudi Arabia: A Practical Guide

Published 16 November 2026 · Qat'atuk team

Finding the right spare part for your car can take days of phone calls and shop visits, especially for newer models or less common parts. This guide covers how to buy car parts in Saudi Arabia with fewer mistakes: what to prepare, how to choose between part types, and how sourcing and shipping work.

1. Know exactly which part you need

Start from the part name and, if you can, the number printed on the old part. If you are unsure, a photo of the part or the damaged area helps a lot. Note whether it is front or rear, left or right.

2. Prepare your car details

  • Make, model and model year (for example Toyota Camry 2019).
  • Trim level and engine type or size.
  • Your VIN: the 17-character number on your registration card, the driver-door sticker, or the plate under the windshield on the driver side.

3. Decide between genuine, aftermarket and used parts

  • Genuine (OEM) parts match the original specification and usually cost the most.
  • Aftermarket parts vary by manufacturer; reputable brands can offer good value, so ask who makes the part.
  • Used parts can be a sensible option for older or hard-to-find items, but condition varies, so inspection and return terms matter.
  • For safety parts such as brake pads, new parts from a trusted source are recommended.

4. Compare offers, not just prices

Ask what is included, whether the part is new or used, the expected delivery time, the shipping cost and the exchange terms. A very low price is worth double-checking against the part number.

5. Shipping and delivery

Parts can be shipped to cities across the Kingdom, including Riyadh, Jeddah, Makkah, Madinah, Dammam, Khobar and Taif. Delivery time depends on whether the part is in the Kingdom or must be imported, and on customs clearance. Inspect the shipment on receipt and report any damage right away.
